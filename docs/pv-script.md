# Vibe Watch — 60-Second PV Script

## Direction

- Shoot landscape in 4K/30 fps with the DJI Osmo Pocket 4P.
- Keep the real startup jingle and OK/NG sound effects audible; lower the music beneath them.
- Use direct cuts and one restrained speed ramp. Avoid decorative transitions.
- Record the English voice-over in a quiet room, close to the microphone.
- Use the short captions below so the story still works with sound muted.

## CapCut Edit Notes

- Place the voice-over first and cut the pictures to its rhythm.
- Target about 95–105 spoken words per minute; leave room for the real device sounds.
- Duck background music to roughly 20% under narration and 10% during device sounds.
- Let the startup jingle, NG sound, and OK sound each play cleanly for at least half a second.
- Use white subtitles with a subtle dark outline; keep them above the bottom caption-safe area.
- Finish with a one-second hold on the Vibe Watch logo and the public repository URL.
